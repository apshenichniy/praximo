Client consent

Shown at onboarding — in the Telegram bot conversation and on the web acceptance page, identical text on both. Captured as a Consent Grant; the text version is stored on the grant.

One thing to agree to

To help {coach} prepare for your sessions, Praximo records and analyses them. Here is exactly what that means:

  1. Your session audio is recorded.
  2. The recordings are analysed by AI. The results go only to {coach} — nobody else sees them.
  3. Audio is deleted 30 days after it is transcribed. Transcripts and analysis results are kept until {coach} deletes them.
  4. Everything is processed in the EU, except the AI analysis, which runs in the US. Those requests pass through Cloudflare AI Gateway, which keeps a log of them.
  5. You can withdraw this consent, or ask for your data to be deleted, at any time — just tell {coach}.

Privacy policy

Nothing is saved until you agree.

Covers all five required elements from docs/spec/privacy-retention.md § “Consent text — required elements”, in order. Element 4 now names the AI Gateway logging, which the earlier draft in prototypes/client-web-flow dropped.

Згода клієнта

Показується під час онбордингу — у розмові з Telegram-ботом і на вебсторінці прийняття запрошення, той самий текст в обох місцях.

Одна річ, на яку потрібна ваша згода

Щоб допомогти {coach} готуватися до ваших сесій, Praximo записує та аналізує їх. Ось що саме це означає:

  1. Аудіо ваших сесій записується.
  2. Записи аналізує штучний інтелект. Результати отримує лише {coach} — більше ніхто їх не бачить.
  3. Аудіо видаляється через 30 днів після розшифрування. Транскрипти та результати аналізу зберігаються, доки {coach} їх не видалить.
  4. Уся обробка відбувається в ЄС, окрім аналізу штучним інтелектом — він виконується у США. Ці запити проходять через Cloudflare AI Gateway, який зберігає їх журнал.
  5. Ви можете відкликати цю згоду або попросити видалити ваші дані будь-коли — просто скажіть про це {coach}.

Політика конфіденційності

Нічого не зберігається, доки ви не погодитесь.

Формулювання навмисно уникають дієслів, що узгоджуються з родом коуча («допомогти {coach} готуватися», а не «щоб {coach} могла») — стать коуча системі невідома. Кнопка «Даю згоду» замість «Погоджуюсь(-лася)».

Показывается при онбординге — в разговоре с Telegram-ботом и на веб-странице принятия приглашения, один и тот же текст в обоих местах.

Одна вещь, на которую нужно ваше согласие

Чтобы помочь {coach} готовиться к вашим сессиям, Praximo записывает и анализирует их. Вот что именно это значит:

Политика конфиденциальности

Ничего не сохраняется, пока вы не согласитесь.

Variants

A · factual (recommended)

This session is recorded and analysed by AI for {coach}.

B · refers back to consent

As you agreed earlier: this session is recorded and analysed by AI for {coach}.

C · benefit-framed

This session is recorded so {coach} gets an AI summary afterwards.

A is the plainest and matches the recording indicator shown in-room. B risks reading as a second consent ask. C softens by leading with the benefit but under-states the analysis.

Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Praximo is a tool that coaches use to schedule sessions, run them as video calls, and get AI-written notes afterwards. This page explains what happens to your data.

Who is responsible for what

Your coach decides what data is collected about you and what happens to it — they are the data controller. We run the software on their instructions — we are the processor. If you want your data changed or deleted, ask your coach; we give them the tools to do it.

Praximo is operated by [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME, ADDRESS].

What we hold

  • Your profile — the name you gave, and optionally a photo, an email address, and your Telegram account id.
  • Session audio — recorded during the call, one track per person.
  • Transcripts and AI notes — the written record of the session and the summaries produced from it.
  • Consent and technical records — the consent you gave, its text version, and when you joined sessions.

If you use Continue with Google on the invitation page, we read your name, photo, email address, and Google account id from your Google profile — only when you click it, and we do not keep the Google access token.

What it is used for

To run the sessions and to produce written notes for your coach: a summary of the session, and a professional self-review addressed to the coach about their own coaching. Nobody but your coach sees these. We do not sell your data, we do not advertise to you, and your recordings and transcripts are not used to train AI models.

Where it is processed

Everything stays in the European Union — hosting, database, video calls, and transcription — except the AI analysis, which runs on providers in the United States. Those requests pass through Cloudflare AI Gateway, which keeps a log of them. The providers are under contracts that forbid training on the data we send.

How long it is kept

  • Session audio — deleted 30 days after it is transcribed.
  • Transcripts and AI notes — until your coach deletes them. They can delete a single session's data or your record entirely.
  • Backups — deleted data can survive in database backups for up to 7 more days, after which it is gone.

Deletion is permanent. There is no undo and no archive.

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, correct it, have it deleted, or withdraw your consent — at any time, and without giving a reason. Ask your coach; they act on it. If your coach cannot help, write to [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will. Withdrawing consent stops new sessions being scheduled; it does not by itself delete what already exists — ask for deletion too if that is what you want.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority in [JURISDICTION].

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access is limited to what is needed to operate the service. If a breach affects you, we tell your coach without undue delay, and they tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, your coach is notified and the version at the top of this page changes. The version you agreed to is recorded with your consent.

Terms of service

1. What Praximo is

Praximo is operated by [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME, ADDRESS] (“we”).

3. Your responsibilities

  • You must have a lawful basis for the client data you put into Praximo, and you must not add clients who have not accepted the consent Praximo presents to them.

The notes are generated by large language models and are assistive, not authoritative. They may contain errors. You are responsible for how you use them with your clients. The competency framework the self-review draws on is described in our own words; we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by any coaching federation.

  • What we process: client profiles, session audio, transcripts, AI notes, consent records — for as long as the retention rules in the privacy policy allow.

The practice data is yours. The software is ours. Nothing here transfers ownership either way.
